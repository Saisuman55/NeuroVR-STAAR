## 3D Medical Imaging and Brain Tumor Segmentation

These papers provide the scientific foundation and comparison context for NeuroVR's planned 3D pipeline. Their reported results belong to the cited studies and must never be presented as NeuroVR performance. Dataset details are recorded only where verified from the source metadata; otherwise they remain explicitly marked for full-text verification.

### NeuroVR 3D Model Selection Rationale

NeuroVR initially adopts a 3D U-Net architecture as a research-grounded baseline for volumetric brain-tumor segmentation. Its encoder-decoder structure, 3D convolutional processing, and skip connections provide a clear and experimentally testable starting point for the project. This is a baseline choice, not a claim that 3D U-Net is the best model. V-Net, nnU-Net, TransBTS, and other architectures remain candidates for later experimental comparison using the project's actual data and evaluation protocol.

### Research progression

The literature progression used for NeuroVR is: **2D segmentation** establishes slice-based baselines and localization ideas; **3D U-Net** extends U-Net to volumetric context; **V-Net** provides a fully convolutional volumetric alternative with overlap-aware learning; **BraTS** supplies a brain-tumor benchmark and evaluation context; **nnU-Net** demonstrates task-adaptive biomedical segmentation configuration; **modern 3D brain-tumor approaches such as TransBTS** add global-context modeling for multimodal MRI; NeuroVR then proposes its own reproducible 3D MRI loading, preprocessing, segmentation, spatial analysis, reconstruction, and visualization pipeline. This progression is background and methodology context, not a claim that NeuroVR has achieved any published result.

## Topics still requiring project-specific evidence

- MRI preprocessing and normalization choices must be justified against the selected dataset and documented experiments.
- Augmentation policies must be evaluated for label preservation and validation leakage.
- Dice, IoU, sensitivity, specificity, and classification metrics must be computed on NeuroVR's own held-out data.
- Explainability and medical visualization claims require additional targeted literature before those features are implemented.
- Two-dimensional slices and three-dimensional NIfTI volumes represent different experimental settings; results must not be compared without a defined protocol.
