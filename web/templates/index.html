B.Tech CSE Major Project / Academic Research Prototype / Not for Clinical Diagnosis

Brain MRI Analysis & 3D Tumor Visualization

NeuroVR is an academic research prototype combining 2D brain tumor classification architecture, volumetric multimodal MRI processing, 3D U-Net segmentation baseline, and an interactive Three.js 3D visualization workspace. All clinical capabilities require trained model checkpoints and verified segmentation masks.
